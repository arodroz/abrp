# Follow Camera behaviour: what Google, Mapbox and the open-source navigators actually do

Research for issue #88 (map #86). Answers the ticket's five questions from primary sources (SDK reference pages and source code on GitHub), then proposes a starting parameter set for the prototype ticket in MapLibre terms. Every claim carries the URL it was read from; facts checked 2026-09-17. Source versions: Mapbox Navigation iOS `v2.22.0` and `v3.27.3`, Mapbox Navigation Android `v2.22.2` / `v3.30.1`, MapLibre Native `ios-v6.29.0`, OsmAnd / Organic Maps / maplibre-navigation-* / Ferrostar / swiftui-dsl at `main` on the check date.

Terms follow `CONTEXT.md`: *Drive Mode*, *Go*, *Follow Camera* (the heading-up camera that follows the snapped position in Drive Mode; today `DriveStore.applyFollowingCamera`, pitch 45°, altitude 800 m, 0.8 s linear).

## 1. Summary / decision facts

| Question | Google (documented) | Mapbox Navigation SDK (source) | Open-source navigators (source) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zoom vs speed | Not documented. An "automatic default zoom" exists and can be overridden (`followingZoomLevel`), formula unpublished | **Not speed-based.** Zoom fits the route ahead over a *lookahead distance* = 7 × average intersection spacing on the current step (spacings < 20 m ignored); clamped to zoom 10.5–16.35 | **Speed-based.** OsmAnd: show `speed × 45 s / 1.4` metres ahead (min 200 m, max zoom 17, Car profile), speed filtered, zoom rate-limited to 0.1 zoom/s, off below 7 km/h. Organic Maps: piecewise-linear m/px table from 20 km/h to 95 km/h. maplibre-navigation-ios: two altitudes, 1000 m / 2000 m on motorway |
| Manoeuvre pull-in | Not documented | Pitch ramps 45° → 0° linearly over the last 180 m before a turn (not for continue/merge/ramp/fork); 100 m of road after the turn is added to the frame; up to 150 m of compound turns coalesced. Release is the same formula recomputed on the next step | OsmAnd: look-ahead distance is capped to the distance to the next turn once the turn is nearer than the speed distance, and held on that turn until passed. maplibre-navigation-android: tilt = clamp(distance/5, 45°, 60°) |
| Framing | iOS SDK: "When guidance is active, the user location is positioned near the bottom of the viewport" | Puck anchored 7 pt above the bottom of the padded viewport (Android `FocalPoint(0.5, 1.0)`); expressed as camera padding `top = anchor.y`, `bottom = height − anchor.y + 1`. CarPlay adds 65 pt (v2) / 45 pt (v3) at the bottom | maplibre-navigation-ios: `edgePadding.top = 0.4 × height` (puck at 70 % down). Organic Maps: 104 dp above the bottom of the visible rect. OsmAnd look-ahead focus at 1/3 from the top |
| Pitch | iOS SDK Following mode: "Changes the view angle to 45 degrees" | 45° default (clamp 0–85), adaptive to 0° at turns; overview 0° | Ferrostar 45° fixed; maplibre-nav-ios 45° fixed; maplibre-nav-android 45–60° by distance; Organic Maps auto-perspective 45° → 55° with zoom; OsmAnd auto-zoom 3D elevation angle 25° (≈ pitch 65°) |
| Heading | "faces the direction of travel"; stationary behaviour undocumented | Bearing = direction from the puck to the end of the lookahead slice of route, clamped to GPS course ± 45°; skip updates < 1° | Organic Maps: GPS bearing only above 0.7 m/s (or when glued to the route), else compass after 3 s. OsmAnd: bearing 0/absent ignored, compass registered below 0.5 m/s. MapLibre built-in: raw `CLLocation.course` |
| Per-fix animation | Not documented | **1.0 s linear** per location update (iOS `UICubicTimingParameters (0,0)-(1,1)`, Android `LINEAR_ANIMATION_DURATION = 1000` ms); centre updates skipped under 2 px, bearing/pitch under 1° | MapLibre built-in `followWithCourse`: 1.0 s, optionally `min(Δt between fixes, 1.0 s)`. maplibre-nav-ios: 1 s linear. OsmAnd: move over the fix interval, rotation linear over ≥ 1000 ms, zoom at 0.1 zoom/s. Organic Maps: animations capped at 1.5 s |

**Bottom line.** Google publishes only 45° and "puck near the bottom"; the *feel* has to be reconstructed from the SDKs that copy it. Two families exist: Mapbox frames *route geometry ahead* (zoom follows intersection density, pitch drops at turns); OsmAnd/Organic Maps zoom by *speed* with a look-ahead-time model and rate limiting. Wayfinder's grilled preference (fixed 60°, lower-third puck, altitude by speed, turns pull in) maps cleanly onto the OsmAnd model with Mapbox's after-turn framing and bearing clamp, animated the way everyone animates: one linear transition per fix, one second long. Section 8 states the numbers.

## 2. Altitude / zoom as a function of speed

### 2.1 Google

- Google's Navigation SDK for iOS documents that a default automatic zoom exists but not what it is: `GMSNavigationNoFollowingZoomLevel` "reverts to the Navigation SDK's automatic default zoom behavior", and `followingZoomLevel` "will override the default Navigation SDK zoom level when the camera is following device location". Source: [GMSNavigationNoFollowingZoomLevel](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/navigation/ios-sdk/reference/objc/Constants/GMSNavigationNoFollowingZoomLevel.html).
- Android `FollowMyLocationOptions.Builder.setZoomLevel(float)` accepts 2–21 inclusive; no default stated. Source: [FollowMyLocationOptions.Builder](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/navigation/android-sdk/reference/com/google/android/gms/maps/model/FollowMyLocationOptions.Builder).
- No Google page states that zoom depends on speed. Consumer forum reports of the map "zooming in and out" while driving are anecdotal and not cited as fact.

### 2.2 Mapbox Navigation SDK: geometry-driven, not speed-driven

- Zoom range: `public var zoomRange: ClosedRange<Double> = 10.50...16.35` (iOS v2 and v3), `minZoom = 10.5`, `maxZoom = 16.35` (Android v2/v3). Sources: [FollowingCameraOptions.swift v2.22.0 L43](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/FollowingCameraOptions.swift#L43), [v3.27.3 L36](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v3.27.3/Sources/MapboxNavigationCore/Map/Camera/FollowingCameraOptions.swift#L36), [MapboxNavigationViewportDataSourceOptions.kt v2.22.2](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-android/blob/v2.22.2/libnavui-maps/src/main/java/com/mapbox/navigation/ui/maps/camera/data/MapboxNavigationViewportDataSourceOptions.kt#L39-L46).
- Zoom is the zoom that fits the *remaining coordinates of the current step, sliced to the lookahead distance*, clamped to the range. `coordinatesForIntersections = coordinatesToManeuver.sliced(from: nil, to: LineString(coordinatesToManeuver).coordinateFromStart(distance: lookaheadDistance))` then `zoom(coordinatesForIntersections, pitch:, maxPitch: defaultPitch, edgeInsets: viewportPadding, defaultZoomLevel: 12.0, maxZoomLevel: zoomRange.upperBound, minZoomLevel: zoomRange.lowerBound)`. Source: [NavigationViewportDataSource.swift v2.22.0 L290-L305](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L290-L305).
- The fit inflates the box to compensate for pitch: `widthDelta = insetHeight * 2 - insetWidth; pitchDelta = (pitch / maxPitch) * widthDelta; heightWithPitchEffect = insetHeight + insetHeight * sin(pitch) * 1.25`. Source: [same file L530-L549](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L530-L549).
- Lookahead distance = average distance between intersections on each step (start, each `step.intersections` location, end), spacings `<= minimumDistanceBetweenIntersections` (20 m) dropped, times `averageDistanceMultiplier` (7.0). Sources: [L598-L625](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L598-L625), [FollowingCameraOptions.swift L192, L202](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/FollowingCameraOptions.swift#L192). This is the indirect speed coupling: motorways have sparse intersections, so the frame is long and the zoom low; towns have dense ones.
- Without a route (free drive) the zoom is a fixed altitude: `let altitude = 4000.0; ZoomLevelForAltitude(altitude, mapView.cameraState.pitch, latitude, mapView.bounds.size)` in v2 ([L175-L179](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L175-L179)); `1700.0` in v3 ([MobileViewportDataSource.swift L80](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v3.27.3/Sources/MapboxNavigationCore/Map/Camera/ViewportDataSource/MobileViewportDataSource.swift#L80)).
- No explicit zoom hysteresis: smoothing comes only from the 1 s per-fix animator (section 6) and from the lookahead being a per-step average (it changes at step boundaries, not per fix).

### 2.3 OsmAnd: look-ahead time, filtered speed, rate-limited zoom

OsmAnd's auto-zoom is on by default for the Car profile (`AUTO_ZOOM_MAP.setModeDefaultValue(ApplicationMode.CAR, true)`, scale `FAR`). Source: [OsmandSettings.java L1590-L1605](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/settings/backend/OsmandSettings.java#L1590-L1605).

- Scale presets `AutoZoomMap(coefficient, maxZoom, minDistanceToDrive)`: `FARTHEST(1f, 16, 400)`, `FAR(1.4f, 17, 200)`, `CLOSE(2f, 19, 50)`. Source: [AutoZoomMap.java](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/settings/enums/AutoZoomMap.java).
- Distance to show ahead (v2 algorithm): `showDistanceToDrive = speed * SHOW_DRIVING_SECONDS_V2 / autoZoomScale.coefficient` with `SHOW_DRIVING_SECONDS_V2 = 45`, floored at `minDistanceToDrive`; with `FAR` that is **speed × 32 s, min 200 m**. Source: [AutoZoomBySpeedHelper.java L55, L235-L252](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/views/AutoZoomBySpeedHelper.java#L235-L252).
- The zoom is whatever makes that distance span from the puck pixel to a *focus pixel* at `FOCUS_PIXEL_RATIO_X = 0.5, FOCUS_PIXEL_RATIO_Y = 1/3` (a third of the way down the screen), computed on the rendered 3D surface (`getSurfaceZoomAfterPinch`), so it is correct at any tilt. Source: [L58-L59, L135-L162](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/views/AutoZoomBySpeedHelper.java#L135-L162).
- Gating and smoothing: no auto-zoom below `MIN_AUTO_ZOOM_SPEED = 7 / 3.6f` m/s (7 km/h); speed goes through a `SpeedFilter` first; the zoom animation runs at `ZOOM_PER_SECOND = 0.1f` and any change whose duration would be under `MIN_ZOOM_DURATION_MILLIS = 1500` (i.e. |Δzoom| < 0.15) is dropped. Source: [L52-L56, L121-L131, L220-L232](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/views/AutoZoomBySpeedHelper.java#L220-L232). This is the hysteresis that stops hunting: small targets are ignored and big ones are approached at 0.1 zoom/s (a full zoom level takes 10 s).
- The v1 (discrete) algorithm shows the same idea with a coarser dead-band: time horizon 60 s below 83 km/h else 75 s, ignore |Δzoom| < 0.5, shave 1 off deltas ≥ 2, round to thirds. Source: [L90-L118](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/views/AutoZoomBySpeedHelper.java#L90-L118).
- After a manual zoom, auto-zoom stays off for `max(AUTO_FOLLOW_ROUTE × 1000, AUTO_ZOOM_DEFAULT_CHANGE_ZOOM = 4500)` ms. Source: [MapViewTrackingUtilities.java L65, L488-L498](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/base/MapViewTrackingUtilities.java#L488-L498).

### 2.4 Organic Maps: piecewise-linear table

`CalculateZoomBySpeed(speedMpS, isPerspectiveAllowed)` returns metres per pixel (divided by the visual scale) by linear interpolation in a `{km/h, m/px}` table, 3D: `{20, 0.25}, {40, 0.75}, {60, 1.50}, {75, 2.50}, {85, 3.75}, {95, 6.00}`; 2D: `{20, 0.70}, {40, 1.25}, {60, 2.25}, {75, 3.00}, {85, 3.75}, {95, 6.00}`; clamped to the ends; unknown speed uses 80 km/h. The scale is applied only in `FollowAndRotate` mode while routing (`UpdateViewportWithAutoZoom`), and is blocked for `kMaxBlockAutoZoomTimeSec = 10.0` s after a user gesture. Source: [my_position_controller.cpp L35, L62-L109, L412-L421, L554-L563](https://github.com/organicmaps/organicmaps/blob/master/libs/drape_frontend/my_position_controller.cpp#L62-L109). Note the 3D table zooms in about 3× further than the 2D one at low speed: tilt shows more road ahead per screen, so the camera can be closer.

### 2.5 MapLibre navigation forks

- maplibre-navigation-ios: `defaultAltitude = 1000.0`, `zoomedOutMotorwayAltitude = 2000.0`, `longManeuverDistance = 1000.0`; the camera zooms out to 2000 m only when the current and upcoming steps are motorway and the manoeuvre is ≥ 1000 m away, and back to 1000 m on the step's last spoken instruction. CarPlay overrides 1000 m / 500 m. Sources: [NavigationMapView.swift L25-L35](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-ios/blob/main/MapboxNavigation/NavigationMapView.swift#L25-L35), [RouteMapViewController.swift L301-L322](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-ios/blob/main/MapboxNavigation/RouteMapViewController.swift#L301), [CarPlayNavigationViewController.swift L81-L82](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-ios/blob/main/MapboxNavigation/CarPlayNavigationViewController.swift#L81).
- maplibre-navigation-android `DynamicCamera`: `MAX_CAMERA_ZOOM = 16`, `MIN_CAMERA_ZOOM = 12`; zoom fits `[currentLocation, upcomingManeuverLocation]` and is recomputed only on step/alert-level changes. Source: [DynamicCamera.java L25-L28, L111-L122, L192-L199](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-android/blob/main/libandroid-navigation-ui/src/main/java/org/maplibre/navigation/android/navigation/ui/v5/camera/DynamicCamera.java#L25).
- Ferrostar (MapLibre, SwiftUI): `NavigationActivity.automotive` zoom `16.0`, pitch `45.0`, fixed pitch range; no speed logic. Source: [MapViewCamera.swift](https://github.com/stadiamaps/ferrostar/blob/main/apple/Sources/FerrostarMapLibreUI/Extensions/MapViewCamera.swift).

## 3. Manoeuvre pull-in

### 3.1 Mapbox: pitch to zero, frame around the corner

- `PitchNearManeuver`: `enabled = true`, `triggerDistanceToManeuver = 180.0` m. `pitchCoefficient = min(distanceToManeuver, 180) / 180` when the remaining step distance is ≤ 180 m, else 1.0; `pitch = defaultPitch * pitchCoefficient`, so the pitch ramps linearly from 45° at 180 m to 0° at the turn. Ignored for upcoming manoeuvre types `[.continue, .merge, .takeOnRamp, .takeOffRamp, .reachFork]` and for the step before arrival. Sources: [FollowingCameraOptions.swift L301-L328](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/FollowingCameraOptions.swift#L315), [NavigationViewportDataSource.swift L562-L586](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L562-L586). Android has the same 180 m / excluded list ([Options.kt L196-L230](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-android/blob/v2.22.2/libnavui-maps/src/main/java/com/mapbox/navigation/ui/maps/camera/data/MapboxNavigationViewportDataSourceOptions.kt#L196-L230)).
- `GeometryFramingAfterManeuver`: `distanceToCoalesceCompoundManeuvers = 150.0` m, `distanceToFrameAfterManeuver = 100.0` m: the first 100 m of the next step (or the whole of several short steps up to 150 m) are appended to the framed coordinates. Sources: [FollowingCameraOptions.swift L275-L282](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/FollowingCameraOptions.swift#L275), [NavigationViewportDataSource.swift L231-L262](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L231-L262).
- The camera *centre* also slides: it moves from the puck toward the centre of the framed box by `(1 − pitchCoefficient)` of the way, while the *anchor* (where the puck is drawn) moves from the bottom toward the middle of the padded view (section 4). Source: [L266-L286, L551-L560](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L266-L286).
- Release: there is no release logic. Once the step advances, `distanceRemaining` on the new step is > 180 m, the coefficient is 1.0 again and the next 1 s per-fix animation carries pitch, centre and anchor back. In practice that is a 1 s tilt-up after the turn.

### 3.2 OsmAnd: look-ahead capped to the next turn

`getShowDistanceToDrive`: if the next turn is nearer than the speed distance, `showDistanceToDrive = nextTurn.distanceTo` and that turn is remembered as `nextTurnInFocus`; while it stays the next turn the look-ahead keeps tracking its distance (so the camera keeps zooming in as the turn approaches, floored at `minDistanceToDrive` = 200 m for `FAR`); a manual zoom or touch clears the focus. Source: [AutoZoomBySpeedHelper.java L235-L252, L269-L282](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/views/AutoZoomBySpeedHelper.java#L235-L282). Release after the turn is the same rate-limited return to the speed distance (0.1 zoom/s), so zooming back out one level takes about 10 s.

### 3.3 Others

- maplibre-navigation-android: `createTilt = clamp(distanceRemaining / 5, MIN_CAMERA_TILT = 45, MAX_CAMERA_TILT = 60)`: 60° when the manoeuvre is > 300 m away, falling to 45° by 225 m. Zoom is refit to `[location, manoeuvre]` on step changes. Source: [DynamicCamera.java L25-L28, L92-L100](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-android/blob/main/libandroid-navigation-ui/src/main/java/org/maplibre/navigation/android/navigation/ui/v5/camera/DynamicCamera.java#L92).
- maplibre-navigation-ios: the two-altitude switch of 2.5 is its only pull-in.
- Google: nothing documented (searched the iOS and Android Navigation SDK camera guides and enum references).

## 4. Framing

### 4.1 Where the vehicle sits

- Google iOS SDK, `GMSNavigationCameraPerspective` Tilted and TopDownHeadingUp: "When guidance is inactive, the user location is positioned at the center of the viewport. When guidance is active, the user location is positioned near the bottom of the viewport." TopDownNorthUp: "The user location is positioned at the center of the screen." Source: [GMSNavigationCameraPerspective](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/navigation/ios-sdk/reference/objc/Enums/GMSNavigationCameraPerspective.html). No numeric offset and no portrait/landscape distinction anywhere in the Navigation SDK docs.
- Mapbox iOS: `anchor(pitchCoefficient, bounds, edgeInsets)` puts the puck at `yCenter + (height/2 − 7) × pitchCoefficient` of the padded area, i.e. **7 pt above the bottom of the padded viewport** away from turns, rising to the padded centre as the pitch drops to 0. Source: [NavigationViewportDataSource.swift L551-L560](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L551-L560). Mapbox Android: `focalPoint = FocalPoint(0.5, 1.0)` (bottom edge of the padded rect). Source: [Options.kt L62](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-android/blob/v2.22.2/libnavui-maps/src/main/java/com/mapbox/navigation/ui/maps/camera/data/MapboxNavigationViewportDataSourceOptions.kt#L62). "Padded" is the key word: `viewportPadding` is the space taken by the manoeuvre banner at the top and the bottom bar, so the puck sits just above the bottom UI.
- maplibre-navigation-ios: `edgePadding.top = bounds.height * 0.4 − safeAreaInsets.bottom`, so the centre of the remaining 60 % is at 70 % of the height. Source: [NavigationMapView.swift L283-L299](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-ios/blob/main/MapboxNavigation/NavigationMapView.swift#L283-L299).
- Organic Maps: `GetRoutingRotationPixelCenter() = {center.x, visiblePixelRect.maxY() − kPositionRoutingOffsetY}` with `kPositionRoutingOffsetY = 104` dp. Source: [my_position_controller.cpp L24, L756-L759](https://github.com/organicmaps/organicmaps/blob/master/libs/drape_frontend/my_position_controller.cpp#L756).
- OsmAnd's look-ahead focus pixel is at 1/3 from the top (2.3); its own puck placement is a user setting not read here.
- Ferrostar's portrait view: "The user puck and route are optimized for the center of the screen"; its docs say the default navigation camera "keeps the location puck toward the bottom of the view" and that top padding moves it. Sources: [PortraitNavigationView.swift L31-L34](https://github.com/stadiamaps/ferrostar/blob/main/apple/Sources/FerrostarMapLibreUI/Views/PortraitNavigationView.swift#L31), [Ferrostar book](https://stadiamaps.github.io/ferrostar/jetpack-compose-customization.html).

### 4.2 How it is expressed

- Mapbox turns the anchor into camera padding: `padding = UIEdgeInsets(top: anchor.y, left: viewportPadding.left, bottom: bounds.height − anchor.y + 1.0, right: viewportPadding.right)` so that the padded viewport's centre *is* the puck. Source: [L367-L372](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L367-L372).
- MapLibre's equivalent is `MLNMapView.contentInset` ("if the only the top edge is inset, the map center is effectively shifted downward"; persistent) or the per-call `edgePadding` of `setCamera(_:withDuration:animationTimingFunction:edgePadding:completionHandler:)` (transient); `cameraEdgeInsets` (read-only) is their sum. Source: [MLNMapView.h ios-v6.29.0](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/platform/ios/src/MLNMapView.h). MapLibre's own tracking modes place the puck through `MLNMapViewDelegate.mapViewUserLocationAnchorPoint:` (the older `userLocationVerticalAlignment` is deprecated in 6.29). Source: [MLNMapView.mm `userLocationAnnotationViewCenter`](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/platform/ios/src/MLNMapView.mm).
- Landscape / CarPlay: Mapbox uses the same anchor formula with a wider padding: CarPlay `carPlayCameraPadding = safeArea + centerEdgeInsets; bottom += 65.0` (v2, "at the same level with trip estimate view"), `+= 45.0` in v3. Sources: [L224-L228](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L224-L228), [CarPlayViewportDataSource.swift v3 L121](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v3.27.3/Sources/MapboxNavigationCore/Map/Camera/ViewportDataSource/CarPlayViewportDataSource.swift#L121). Ferrostar's landscape view puts "the instructions on the leading half of the screen and the user puck and route on the trailing half". Source: [LandscapeNavigationView.swift L26-L28](https://github.com/stadiamaps/ferrostar/blob/main/apple/Sources/FerrostarMapLibreUI/Views/LandscapeNavigationView.swift#L26). The CarPlay App Programming Guide only constrains the base view ("must be used exclusively to draw a map") and gestures; it says nothing about camera placement. Source: [CarPlay App Programming Guide (PDF)](https://developer.apple.com/carplay/documentation/CarPlay-App-Programming-Guide.pdf), "Additional guidelines for CarPlay navigation apps".

## 5. Pitch and heading

### 5.1 Pitch values in practice

| Implementation | Pitch | Adaptive? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Navigation SDK iOS, Following | 45° | not documented | [camera guide](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/navigation/ios-sdk/camera): "Changes the view angle to 45 degrees and puts the camera behind the current position facing in the direction of travel" |
| Mapbox iOS/Android | `defaultPitch = 45.0` (clamp 0–85) | yes: × pitchCoefficient, 0° at turns, 0° overview | [FollowingCameraOptions.swift L19](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/FollowingCameraOptions.swift#L19) |
| maplibre-navigation-ios | 45° fixed | no | [NavigationMapView.swift L283-L299](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-ios/blob/main/MapboxNavigation/NavigationMapView.swift#L283) |
| maplibre-navigation-android | 45°–60° | yes, by distance to manoeuvre; `DEFAULT_TILT = 50` idle | [DynamicCamera.java](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-android/blob/main/libandroid-navigation-ui/src/main/java/org/maplibre/navigation/android/navigation/ui/v5/camera/DynamicCamera.java#L25), [SimpleCamera.kt](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-android/blob/main/maplibre-navigation-core/src/commonMain/kotlin/org/maplibre/navigation/core/navigation/camera/SimpleCamera.kt) |
| Ferrostar | 45° fixed (`pitchRange: .fixed`) | no | [MapViewCamera.swift](https://github.com/stadiamaps/ferrostar/blob/main/apple/Sources/FerrostarMapLibreUI/Extensions/MapViewCamera.swift) |
| Organic Maps auto-perspective | 45° (`kMaxPerspectiveAngle1 = π/4`) rising to 55° (`kMaxPerspectiveAngle2`) as scale passes `kEndPerspectiveScale1` | yes, by zoom | [screenbase.cpp L8-L15, L95-L109](https://github.com/organicmaps/organicmaps/blob/master/libs/geometry/screenbase.cpp#L8) |
| OsmAnd auto-zoom 3D | `AUTO_ZOOM_3D_ANGLE = 25` (elevation angle, 90 = flat, so ≈ pitch 65°) | applied while auto-zooming in 3D | [OsmandSettings.java L1608](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/settings/backend/OsmandSettings.java#L1608), [OsmandMapTileView.java L91](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/views/OsmandMapTileView.java#L91) |

The grilled 60° is at the steep end of shipped practice (Mapbox/Google/Ferrostar 45°, maplibre-android up to 60°, OsmAnd 65°), and everyone who adapts pitch flattens it *at* the turn or at high zoom, not on the motorway. Adaptive pitch stays out of scope per the map; the one thing worth knowing for the prototype: MapLibre 6.29's `maximumPitch` doc says "The pitch may not exceed 60 degrees regardless of this property", but `TransformState::setMaxPitch` clamps against `PITCH_MAX = M_PI`, with `DEFAULT_PITCH_MAX = M_PI / 3` only as the default. Whether > 60° renders correctly on iOS 6.29 is unverified. Sources: [MLNMapView.h](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/platform/ios/src/MLNMapView.h), [constants.hpp L34-L36](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/include/mln/util/constants.hpp#L34-L36), [transform_state.cpp L579-L587](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/src/mln/map/transform_state.cpp#L579-L587).

### 5.2 Heading source, smoothing, low speed

- Mapbox: bearing starts from `location.course`; the direction from the puck to the end of the lookahead slice (at least `distanceToCoalesceCompoundManeuvers` = 150 m of route) is computed; if that direction differs from the course by more than `maximumBearingSmoothingAngle = 45.0°` the bearing is `course ± 45°`, otherwise the bearing *is* the route direction. Sources: [FollowingCameraOptions.swift L240](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/FollowingCameraOptions.swift#L240), [NavigationViewportDataSource.swift L318-L332, L492-L512](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationViewportDataSource.swift#L492-L512). Effect: the map is route-locked (no GPS course jitter) but cannot point more than 45° away from where the GPS says the car is going. Per-fix bearing changes under 1° are not animated ([NavigationCameraStateTransition.swift L98, L135](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationCameraStateTransition.swift#L135)). No speed gate: Mapbox trusts the provider. Walking steps use the compass instead (L335-L345).
- Organic Maps: GPS bearing is used only when the compass is unavailable, the arrow is "glued" to the route, or `info.m_speed > kMinSpeedThresholdMps = 0.7` m/s ("2.5 km/h"); afterwards compass updates are ignored for `kGpsBearingLifetimeSec = 3.0` s, then the compass takes over (so a stopped car with no compass keeps its last heading). Source: [my_position_controller.cpp L27-L30, L423-L433, L533-L552](https://github.com/organicmaps/organicmaps/blob/master/libs/drape_frontend/my_position_controller.cpp#L423-L433).
- OsmAnd: rotation = `−location.getBearing()` only when `hasBearing()` and bearing ≠ 0 ("special case when bearing equals to zero (we don't change anything)"); the compass sensor is registered when `isSmallSpeedForCompass` (`!hasSpeed || speed < 0.5` m/s); location animation is treated as small-speed below 1.5 m/s. Source: [MapViewTrackingUtilities.java L301-L321, L351-L357](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/base/MapViewTrackingUtilities.java#L301-L357).
- MapLibre built-in `followWithCourse`: `direction = self.userLocation.location.course` with no extra gating; negative course (CoreLocation's "invalid") simply leaves the direction unchanged. Source: [MLNMapView.mm `directionByFollowingWithCourse`](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/platform/ios/src/MLNMapView.mm).
- CoreLocation: "A negative value indicates that the course information is invalid" ([CLLocation.course](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/corelocation/cllocation/course)); `courseAccuracy` negative also means invalid ([courseAccuracy](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/corelocation/cllocation/courseaccuracy)). Apple documents no speed threshold for course invalidation and no fixed update rate; 1 Hz is observed, not contracted.
- Google: "During navigation, the camera automatically faces the direction of travel" ([Android camera guide](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/navigation/android-sdk/camera)); stationary behaviour and smoothing are not documented.
- Wayfinder today: target = `location.course` if ≥ 0 else the snapped segment bearing, stepped along the shortest arc capped at ±45° per fix (`DriveStore.stepCourse`, ADR 0012 decision 3). That is already the Mapbox clamp value, applied per fix rather than against the route direction.

## 6. Animation per fix

- Mapbox iOS `update(to:state:)` (every location update, following and overview): `let duration = 1.0`, `UICubicTimingParameters(controlPoint1: (0, 0), controlPoint2: (1, 1))` (linear); centre is re-animated only if it moved more than `2.0` px at the current zoom, bearing/pitch only if they changed by ≥ `1.0`°; a running animator for the same property is stopped and replaced. Same in v3. Sources: [NavigationCameraStateTransition.swift v2 L85-L193](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationCameraStateTransition.swift#L85-L193), [v3 L67-L191](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v3.27.3/Sources/MapboxNavigationCore/Map/Camera/NavigationCameraStateTransition.swift#L67-L191).
- Mapbox Android: `transitionLinear` animators use `LINEAR_ANIMATION_DURATION = 1000L` ms with `PathInterpolatorCompat(0, 0, 1, 1)`; `DEFAULT_FRAME_TRANSITION_OPT = maxDuration(1000L)`, `DEFAULT_STATE_TRANSITION_OPT = maxDuration(3500L)`. Sources: [MapboxNavigationCameraTransition.kt](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-android/blob/v2.22.2/libnavui-maps/src/main/java/com/mapbox/navigation/ui/maps/camera/transition/MapboxNavigationCameraTransition.kt), [NavigationCamera.kt L119-L122](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-android/blob/v2.22.2/libnavui-maps/src/main/java/com/mapbox/navigation/ui/maps/camera/NavigationCamera.kt#L119-L122).
- Mapbox state transitions (re-centre after free-look) are the only eased ones: v2 zoom-in centre `clamp(distance / 1500 m/s, 0.6, 1.6)` s, zoom `clamp(Δzoom / 3 levels/s, 0.6, 1.6)` s, bearing `clamp(Δbearing / 60°/s, 0.6, 1.2)` s, pitch 0.8 s, cubic beziers `(0.4,0,0.6,1)` / `(0.2,0,0.6,1)` / `(0.6,0,0.4,1)`; v3 collapses this to `fly(to:duration:)` with 0.5 s zooming in / 0.25 s zooming out. Sources: [v2 L212-L302, L325-L364](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v2.22.0/Sources/MapboxNavigation/NavigationCameraStateTransition.swift#L212-L302), [v3 L33-L54](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-navigation-ios/blob/v3.27.3/Sources/MapboxNavigationCore/Map/Camera/NavigationCameraStateTransition.swift#L33-L54).
- MapLibre Native's own course tracking: `MLNUserLocationAnimationDuration = 1.0` ("chosen to match a typical interval between user location updates"); with `dynamicNavigationCameraAnimationDuration` the duration becomes `MIN(newLocation.timestamp − oldLocation.timestamp, 1.0)`. Source: [MLNMapView.mm L276-L278, L6271-L6276](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/platform/ios/src/MLNMapView.mm). `setCamera(_:withDuration:animationTimingFunction:)` maps to `Map::easeTo` with the timing function converted to a `UnitBezier` (nil = `kCAMediaTimingFunctionDefault`); a new `easeTo` replaces the in-flight transition's frame function without calling `cancelTransitions()`, so back-to-back per-fix calls chain from the current mid-animation state (the superseded call's completion handler never fires). Sources: [MLNMapView.mm `MLNUnitBezierForMediaTimingFunction`](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/platform/ios/src/MLNMapView.mm), [transform.cpp `Transform::easeTo` / `cancelTransitions`](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/src/mln/map/transform.cpp). Animation is driven by the view's `CADisplayLink` at `preferredFramesPerSecond`.
- maplibre-navigation-ios: `setCamera(camera, withDuration: 1, animationTimingFunction: CAMediaTimingFunction(name: .linear))` per progress update. Source: [NavigationMapView.swift L283-L299](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-ios/blob/main/MapboxNavigation/NavigationMapView.swift#L283-L299).
- maplibre-navigation-android: zoom animation `clamp(500 × |Δzoom|, 300, 1500)` ms, tilt `clamp(500 × |Δtilt|, 750, 1500)` ms (`NAVIGATION_MAX_CAMERA_ADJUSTMENT_ANIMATION_DURATION = 1500L`). Sources: [NavigationCamera.java L541-L555](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-android/blob/main/libandroid-navigation-ui/src/main/java/org/maplibre/navigation/android/navigation/ui/v5/camera/NavigationCamera.java#L541), [NavigationConstants.kt L70-L80](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-navigation-android/blob/main/maplibre-navigation-core/src/commonMain/kotlin/org/maplibre/navigation/core/navigation/NavigationConstants.kt#L70).
- OsmAnd: with `ANIMATE_MY_LOCATION`, the move duration is the time since the previous fix (`movingTime = timeDiff`), else `min(timeDiff × 0.7, MOVE_ANIMATION_TIME = 500)`; the target animation lasts `max(movingTime, NAV_ANIMATION_TIME / 4)` with `NAV_ANIMATION_TIME = 1000f`; rotation is linear over `max(animationDuration, ROTATION_MOVE_ANIMATION_TIME = 1000f)`; animation is skipped when `movingTime > SKIP_ANIMATION_TIMEOUT = 10000` ms. Zoom runs at 0.1 zoom/s (2.3). It also animates toward a `predictedLocation` (dead-reckoned) when one is available. Sources: [MapViewTrackingUtilities.java L64, L358-L406](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/base/MapViewTrackingUtilities.java#L358-L406), [AnimateDraggingMapThread.java L44-L49, L340-L400](https://github.com/osmandapp/OsmAnd/blob/master/OsmAnd/src/net/osmand/plus/views/AnimateDraggingMapThread.java#L340-L400).
- Organic Maps: follow animations are capped at `kMaxAnimationTimeSec = 1.5` s. Source: [animation_constants.hpp](https://github.com/organicmaps/organicmaps/blob/master/libs/drape_frontend/animation_constants.hpp).
- Google: nothing documented about update animation; the iOS guide's re-centre example uses a five-second timer after a pan ([iOS camera guide](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/navigation/ios-sdk/camera)).

The consensus is unanimous: **one linear animation per fix whose duration equals the fix interval (1 s)**, so the map never stops moving between fixes and never overshoots. Wayfinder's 0.8 s leaves a 0.2 s hold every second.

## 7. MapLibre 6.29 facts that shape the numbers

- `MLNMapCamera.altitude` is "the distance from the viewpoint to the map, perpendicular to the map plane", i.e. eye height, and `viewingDistance = altitude / cos(pitch)`. Source: [MLNMapCamera.h / .mm](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/platform/darwin/src/MLNMapCamera.h). Prefer `MLNMapCamera(lookingAtCenter:altitude:pitch:heading:)` (the `fromDistance:` initialiser is deprecated for misreading its argument).
- Zoom ↔ altitude: `MLNAltitudeForZoomLevel(zoom, pitch, latitude, size)` = `metersPerPixel(lat, zoom) × size.height / 2 / tan(MLNAngularFieldOfView / 2) × cos(pitch)` with `MLNAngularFieldOfView = 30°`; the inverse is `MLNZoomLevelForAltitude`. Altitude therefore depends on the view height and latitude; the prototype should compute altitude from a target zoom at runtime rather than hard-code metres. Source: [MLNGeometry.mm L17, L50-L66](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/platform/darwin/src/MLNGeometry.mm).
- Reference table (852 pt tall view, latitude 50°, 512 px tiles):

  | zoom | m/pt | altitude @ pitch 60° | @ 45° | @ 0° |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 14.0 | 3.07 | 2441 m | 3452 m | 4882 m |
  | 14.5 | 2.17 | 1726 m | 2441 m | 3452 m |
  | 15.0 | 1.54 | 1221 m | 1726 m | 2441 m |
  | 15.5 | 1.09 | 863 m | 1221 m | 1726 m |
  | 16.0 | 0.77 | 610 m | 863 m | 1221 m |
  | 16.5 | 0.54 | 432 m | 610 m | 863 m |
  | 17.0 | 0.38 | 305 m | 432 m | 610 m |

  Today's 800 m at 45° is zoom ≈ 16.1 by this formula; the same 800 m at 60° would be zoom ≈ 15.6 (further out).
- `contentInset` is persistent and shifts `centerCoordinate`; `edgePadding` on `setCamera(...)` is transient; both accumulate into `cameraEdgeInsets`. `automaticallyAdjustsContentInset` adds bars automatically (keep it off in Drive Mode and set the insets explicitly). Source: [MLNMapView.h](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native/blob/ios-v6.29.0/platform/ios/src/MLNMapView.h).
- Pitch: default `maximumPitch` 60°; see 5.1 for the doc/code discrepancy.

## 8. Recommended starting parameters for the prototype

Expressed for `DriveStore.applyFollowingCamera` and the MapLibre API. Each line names the source it is lifted from; numbers are starting points for the Trip Log replay judgement, not decisions.

**Fixed**
- `pitch = 60` (grilled preference; steepest of shipped practice, OsmAnd's 65° being the only steeper one).
- Heading-up; `heading` computed as below.

**Framing (puck in the lower third)**
- `mapView.automaticallyAdjustsContentInset = false`; `contentInset = UIEdgeInsets(top: 0.4 × H + bottomHUD, left: 0, bottom: bottomHUD, right: 0)` where `H` is the map view height and `bottomHUD` the height of the drive card. With `lookingAtCenter: snappedCoordinate` this puts the puck at 70 % of the height, matching maplibre-navigation-ios (`edgePadding.top = 0.4 × height`) and within Mapbox's "bottom of the padded viewport" and Google's "near the bottom of the viewport". Persistent `contentInset`, not per-call `edgePadding`, so gestures, `convert(_:toCoordinateFrom:)` and the re-centre share the same viewport.
- Landscape: keep the 70 % vertical rule (H is then ≈ 393 pt, so the puck is ≈ 118 pt above the bottom, close to Organic Maps' 104 dp) and add `left = width of the manoeuvre panel` (Ferrostar/Mapbox CarPlay pattern).

**Altitude from speed (OsmAnd model, Mapbox clamp)**
- Filtered speed `v` (EMA over ≈ 3 fixes; OsmAnd filters before mapping).
- Look-ahead distance `D = clamp(v × 32 s, 200 m, 1500 m)` (OsmAnd `FAR`: 45 s / 1.4, floor 200 m; the 1500 m cap keeps the zoom inside the range below).
- Target zoom: the zoom at which `D` metres of route ahead span from the puck to the focus point at 1/3 of the view height (OsmAnd `FOCUS_PIXEL_RATIO_Y = 1/3`). Two ways to get it: measure the ground distance between the puck's screen point and the focus point with `mapView.convert(_:toCoordinateFrom:)` at the current camera and apply `Δzoom = log2(currentDistanceAhead / D)` (OsmAnd `Zoom.fromDistanceRatio`), or use the reference table above as a first cut: roughly zoom 17.0 at ≤ 20 km/h, 16.0 at 50 km/h, 15.25 at 80 km/h, 14.5 at ≥ 120 km/h, interpolated linearly.
- Clamp `zoom ∈ [14.5, 17.0]` (Mapbox's 16.35 ceiling is for 45°; 17 at 60° shows about the same road ahead; OsmAnd `FAR` caps at 17). Convert with `MLNAltitudeForZoomLevel(zoom, 60, lat, mapView.bounds.size)` and pass it as `altitude`.
- Hysteresis: ignore target changes with `|Δzoom| < 0.15` and move the commanded zoom at most `0.1 zoom per second` toward the target (OsmAnd `ZOOM_PER_SECOND = 0.1`, `MIN_ZOOM_DURATION_MILLIS = 1500`). At 1 Hz that is a 0.1 step per fix, so a full zoom level takes 10 s and speed noise cannot hunt. Freeze the zoom below `7 km/h` (OsmAnd `MIN_AUTO_ZOOM_SPEED`) and for 10 s after any user gesture (Organic Maps `kMaxBlockAutoZoomTimeSec`; the existing free-look already exits Follow Camera, so this only matters for a future pinch-while-following).

**Manoeuvre pull-in (OsmAnd cap + Mapbox after-turn framing)**
- Let `dTurn` be the along-route distance to the next manoeuvre point. When `dTurn + 100 m < D`, set `D = max(dTurn + 100 m, 200 m)` (100 m after the turn is Mapbox `distanceToFrameAfterManeuver`; 200 m is the floor) and hold that manoeuvre in focus until it is passed. The same 0.1 zoom/s limiter shapes the pull-in, so at 80 km/h (D ≈ 710 m) the camera starts tightening ≈ 600 m (≈ 27 s) out and can follow the shrinking distance all the way to the floor.
- Skip the pull-in for continue / merge / on-ramp / off-ramp / fork manoeuvres and for the arrival step (Mapbox exclusion list) if the Rust step types expose them; otherwise pull in on every step boundary and judge on replay.
- Release: nothing special. Once the manoeuvre is passed, `dTurn` refers to the next one, `D` returns to the speed distance and the limiter zooms out at 0.1 zoom/s (≈ 10–15 s), which is what OsmAnd does and what the Mapbox 1 s tilt-up approximates.
- Pitch stays 60° through the turn (fixed-pitch preference; Mapbox's 45° → 0° ramp over the last 180 m is the alternative to revisit if the prototype shows the corner badly at 60°).

**Heading**
- Target = bearing from the snapped point to the point `max(150 m, D / 2)` further along the route (Mapbox route-lock, `distanceToCoalesceCompoundManeuvers = 150 m`), clamped to within ±45° of `location.course` when `course >= 0` and `speed > 0.7 m/s` (Mapbox `maximumBearingSmoothingAngle`; Organic Maps `kMinSpeedThresholdMps`). Below 0.7 m/s or with an invalid course: hold the last heading (do not swing to compass; the car has not turned). Keep the existing ±45°/fix step as the outer guard; skip the update when |Δheading| < 1° (Mapbox threshold).

**Animation**
- `setCamera(camera, withDuration: min(Δt since previous fix, 1.0), animationTimingFunction: CAMediaTimingFunction(name: .linear))` on every fix (MapLibre's own `dynamicNavigationCameraAnimationDuration` rule; Mapbox 1.0 s linear). Replace the current 0.8 s. Centre, heading, altitude change together in the one call; MapLibre's `easeTo` chains from the mid-animation state, so no explicit cancellation is needed.
- Snap without animation when the fix is stale (> 10 s since the previous one, OsmAnd `SKIP_ANIMATION_TIMEOUT`) or when re-entering Follow Camera from free-look; for that re-centre use a 0.5 s ease (Mapbox v3 `fly(to:duration: 0.5)`).

**Not recommended for the first prototype**: Mapbox's intersection-density zoom (needs per-step intersection lists the Rust router does not emit), adaptive pitch, dead-reckoned `predictedLocation` (OsmAnd).

## 9. Open points and unverified items

- Google's actual consumer-app speed→zoom and turn behaviour remain undocumented; only the 45° and "near the bottom of the viewport" statements are citable. Anything more is observation from the user's own drives.
- Apple documents no speed threshold for `CLLocation.course` invalidity and no update rate; the 0.7 m/s gate and 1 Hz assumption come from Organic Maps and MapLibre's comment respectively.
- MapLibre 6.29 may accept `maximumPitch > 60` despite its doc comment; not tested on device.
- OsmAnd's `SpeedFilter` constants and its puck-position setting were not read; the EMA-over-3-fixes suggestion is a placeholder.
- `docs.mapbox.com` camera guides were not fetched; all Mapbox numbers come from tagged source, which is what runs.
- CarPlay HIG page body could not be fetched; the App Programming Guide PDF was read and contains no camera guidance.
